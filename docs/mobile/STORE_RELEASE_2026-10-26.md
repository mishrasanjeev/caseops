Choose the real production HTTPS callback only after organization/domain/IDs are established. Implement and prove the matching client callback/parser, backend exact registry, iOS associated-domain entitlement and `apple-app-site-association`, plus Android verified intent filters and `/.well-known/assetlinks.json`. Follow [Apple universal links](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-universal-links-in-your-app) and [Android verification](https://developer.android.com/training/app-links/verify-applinks).

Retain PKCE, state binding, single-use codes, separate native token realm, MFA, strict TLS and logout/revocation fences. A production callback must pass real end-user verification on both signed platforms before any enablement/deployment claim. No flags or auth policy were weakened in this review.
