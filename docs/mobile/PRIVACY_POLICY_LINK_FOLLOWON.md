# App privacy-policy link follow-on

This is a reviewable follow-on, not a published policy or deletion protocol.
The existing publisher contact is owner-supplied and publicly corroborated; do
not replace it with a guessed mailbox. Mailbox delivery, support operation and
authority are separate observations. The private corporate Site remains private.

At the preserved `cfcb98d7` baseline, `AccountHelp.tsx` provides useful privacy text and explicit mail drafts,
but has no accessible HTTPS link to the full app/backend privacy policy. The
repository's `/demo/privacy` concerns public leads only and must not be reused.
An unavailable tool fetch of `/privacy` is not evidence of a 404.

The subsequent, separate Host checkpoint `b1e0efa795e4b710a14704a9b240f36f857235e5` implements the immutable policy reader/plugin and accessible Account link with synthetic controls. It does not supply an approved real URL, page content or owner approval. The packaging executor consumes that reader after integration; this baseline branch refuses real execution when the reader is absent. The engineering proposal below is retained as its review context, not an assertion that the later source feature remains unimplemented.

Prepare a small extension of the existing publisher configuration, with one
owner-approved canonical HTTPS policy URL and policy version. Production build
admission must refuse an absent/unapproved resource. QA may show an honest
unavailable state. The native link must be labeled/accessibly focusable; reject
non-HTTPS, embedded credentials, unapproved origins and
stale callbacks. Do not derive authority from document/source-reference URLs.
Test actual TSX press/recovery and malformed/missing URL controls with synthetic
public inputs before any real URL is selected. No automatic external navigation.

The content decision needs actual app/backend purposes, recipients, processing
regions, document/photo metadata, SecureStore/local-lock handling, retention,
backup/security records, and a functioning rights/support process. Camera
`exif:false` does not strip encoded metadata. Supplier privacy manifests do not
declare the service's entire collection. The approved URL/content and the actual
archive's SDK/manifests/permissions must be reconciled before submission.

The self account-request contract is deliberately receipt-only. Do not add
completion states, purge records or promise a completion period through this
link task. Review the real account-creation/provisioning model and distribution
scope, then approve a separate fulfillment/identity/retention/provider-held-data
policy and external intake where applicable. Legal holds remain intact.

Primary policy references, checked28 October2026:
[Apple privacy5.1.1(i)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/),
[Apple account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion),
[Play deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en).
These references do not certify CaseOps applicability, exemptions or compliance.

No policy publication, account enrollment, real email, production request or
destructive deletion is performed by this preparation.
