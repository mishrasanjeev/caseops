## Source-Reported Workflow

An authorized copyright attorney records the work, author and claimant facts;
versions the deposit; prepares an optional application; and records filing,
deficiency, objection, response, hearing, registration, correction or expungement.
Registration carries its own identifier/date and never creates an ownership
interest. Authorship, ownership, assignment, licence and permission claims retain
distinct parties, periods, source sets and reviewed predecessor links.
Competing claims remain unresolved until an authorized review records its rationale.

Platform takedown, registry and court proceedings remain separate. Notices,
responses and source-reported outcomes retain evidence; a platform response cannot
be stored as a judicial disposition. Closed proceedings cannot be silently reused.
Unreleased works remain restricted regardless of their application stage.

## Shared Safety

Every command rechecks membership/capability, current restricted-docket access,
parent lifecycle and workflow versions and exact source versions under tenant-first
locks. History cannot mutate; revoked access denies history and source retrieval.
Close/reopen retires old commands without resurrecting operational children.
All document links must be authorized. A specialist link keeps general AI, portal,
export and notification unavailable even beside an ordinary trademark link.
No statutory date, provider format, legal status or ownership conclusion is inferred.
