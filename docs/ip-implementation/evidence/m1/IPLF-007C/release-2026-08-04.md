## Scope and ownership

IPLF-007C implements NOTIF-01 through NOTIF-24 and the normal plus two exception
paths of UJ-11. It extends the existing `NotificationDeliveryIntent`,
`HearingReminder`, `EmailSuppression`, Matter hearing, notification preference,
SendGrid webhook, and admin notification owners. It does not create a second
dispatcher, hearing register, scheduler, email connector, or operational deadline
writer.

The slice makes the durable delivery intent the canonical send/outcome record while
retaining `HearingReminder` as a compatibility projection. A lineage table joins a
scheduled hearing reminder to its durable delivery intents. Matter deadlines and
hearings cancel pending intents through the existing lifecycle transaction.

## Implemented behavior

- Hearing time is explicit: `exact`, `session_only`, or `unpublished`. Exact times
  require a valid IANA timezone; non-exact hearings use the disclosed policy time
  and never invent a hidden 10:00 hearing time.
- Hearing policy persists recipient memberships, channels, offsets, criticality,
  escalation memberships, timezone, and the date-only reminder time.
- Scheduling produces immutable recipient/destination snapshots and a destination
  version. External content uses the minimal confidentiality mode.
- Every target is exactly one active membership, portal user, or external reference.
  Tenant and current-access checks occur at enqueue and immediately before send.
- `scheduled_for` is respected by the dispatcher. Retryable and non-retryable
  failures are distinct; provider-disabled delivery remains fail-closed.
- Critical external delivery always creates an in-app companion/fallback when an
  eligible membership exists. External delivery is not silently represented as
  successful.
- SendGrid events are append-only and idempotent even without an event identifier.
  Provider occurrence time and terminal-state precedence prevent a late `delivered`
  event from overwriting a bounce, suppression, cancellation, or dead letter.
- Bounce, suppression, failure, cancellation, retry, and delivery remain distinct
  statuses. Suppression evidence records provider/category, first/last occurrence,
  affected address, fallback, and recovery.
- Recovery is preview-first and creates a successor intent with a new destination
  version. It never rewrites original attempts or provider events. Permanent-bounce
  recovery requires a changed active destination.
- The current user can create a safe in-app test notification; the endpoint records
  zero external calls. Admin operators receive due/attempted/delivered/failure,
  suppression, bounce, fallback, stale-queue, and critical-alert metrics.
- Closing a Matter deadline or changing/cancelling a hearing neutralizes pending
  notification work atomically; no child is resurrected by legacy projection.

## Persistence and migration

Migration `20260804_0004_notification_recovery_convergence` is additive. It expands
hearing policy/time precision, durable intent target/version/scheduling/recovery
metadata, suppression recovery evidence, provider event idempotency/application
metadata, and reminder-to-intent lineage. Existing reminder rows are backfilled into
durable intents and linked without sending. The migration completed from a clean
database during API startup. Rollback is schema-only and must not be used after new
intent/event data becomes authoritative; forward repair is the operational recovery
path.
