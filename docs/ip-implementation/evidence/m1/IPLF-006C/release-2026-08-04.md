## Outcome and canonical ownership

This slice closes the remaining repository-controlled statute trust behavior by
extending, rather than duplicating, the existing owners:

- `Statute` and `StatuteSection` remain the canonical statute catalogue and
  provision records;
- the shared source-action boundary remains the only open/state/report contract;
- `AuthorityDocument` remains the judgment and authority corpus owner;
- existing drafting and litigation-strategy services remain the legal-workflow
  consumers and now read only usable verified statute text;
- `AuditEvent` remains the audit ledger; and
- `StatuteSourceVersion` and `StatuteSourceConflict` add immutable proposal and
  conflict evidence. They are not a second mutable statute catalogue.

The change is classified `EXTEND`. It does not create another research index,
document store, credential store, provider proxy, audit ledger, or legal advice
engine.

## Trust contract implemented

### Fail-closed statutory text

Statutory text is user-visible and consumable only when all of the following are
true: the record is `verified_official` or `verified_licensed`; a SHA-256 is
present; the source is a provision-level deep link; the exact source version is
recorded; legal status is enacted; and the latest link-health state is
`available`. A URL by itself never confers verification.

Unverified, missing, broken, protected, withdrawn, conflicting, quarantined, and
provisional sources preserve citation/provenance context but do not render as
verified statutory text or flow into drafting and litigation strategy.
Unavailable text is labelled accurately, with the reason and recovery action.

Default statute-section lists expose only usable verified sections. Catalogue
counts and verified-available counts are separate, so the UI no longer presents
seed coverage as authoritative coverage.

### Full provenance and source history

Acts and sections now carry issuing body, publisher, source category/status,
legal and verification status, publication/effective dates, retrieval timestamp,
content hash, amendment metadata, history status, exact source version, locator
type, source-policy metadata, link-health status, last check, and last error.

The UI distinguishes the Act landing page from the provision deep link. It shows
statutory text, editorial notes, case annotations, and AI explanation in separate
labelled panels. Editorial or model-generated material cannot overwrite the
statutory-text field.

### Immutable two-person verification

Positive verification can no longer be performed by directly mutating the
canonical section. A curator creates an immutable source-version proposal with
candidate text, hash, exact source/version facts, dates, amendment data, and a
unified diff. The proposal is protected by the current source version.

A different authorized membership must approve or reject with a reason. The
server rejects self-review, stale versions, Act landing pages masquerading as
section links, non-enacted material, unsupported source categories, and sources
whose typed probe is not `available`. Approval atomically applies the canonical
version and supersedes the prior version; rejection leaves canonical text
untouched. The proposal history remains immutable.

Urgent one-person quarantine remains available as a safety action. It can remove
text from operational use immediately but cannot positively verify replacement
text. Controlled supersession still requires independent review.

### Conflict, withdrawal, and link health

Credible conflicts store both source-version facts, authority ranks, evidence,
affected-record references, and impact-scan metadata. Recording a conflict
immediately increments the canonical source version and quarantines the section.
A distinct legal decision records the resolution, while canonical text remains
quarantined until a separately reviewed source proposal supersedes it.

Link probing uses typed outcomes: `available`, `protected`, `missing`,
`rate_limited`, or `unavailable`. Probes use bounded HEAD requests with redirects
disabled and persist check time/error without treating a protected or redirected
page as verified text. The source-report queue from IPLF-004B remains the defect
and retry owner.

### Official, licensed, editorial, and AI sources

Official sources are allow-listed by the shared safe-source policy. Licensed
sources require an explicit internal-use policy with permitted uses; export and
redistribution are denied unless the policy explicitly permits them. The policy
gate is enforced independently of URL shape.

Indian Kanoon enrichment is stored only as editorial notes. India Code discovery
is stored as an unverified official candidate until provision-level two-person
review. Model output is stored only as an AI explanation. Seed/enrichment jobs
cannot downgrade, overwrite, reactivate, or replace verified, licensed,
quarantined, retired, or otherwise reviewed rows.

## API and curator workflow

The statute API now supports:

- creating and listing immutable section source-version proposals;
- independent approve/reject decisions with a mandatory reason;
- explicit typed link checks;
- conflict creation and legal-resolution records; and
- curator search across verified, unverified, and quarantined sections.

The section page provides responsive proposal fields, exact version/source
metadata, candidate text and diff, pending proposal history, review reason, and
Approve/Reject actions. At 360 pixels, all fields and grouped controls are
full-width or wrapping with explicitly shrinkable containers.

## Migration, compatibility, and rollback

Migration `20260804_0003_statute_source_governance` is additive and follows the
single IPLF-005B head `20260804_0002`. It adds provenance, status, history,
policy, and link-health columns to existing statute tables and creates immutable
source-version and conflict tables with indexes and foreign keys.

Existing unverified rows remain readable as catalogue/provenance records but are
fail-closed for statutory text. No migration labels legacy URLs authoritative and
no replacement legal text is fetched or published by the migration.

Mixed-revision compatibility is additive: older application revisions ignore the
new tables and columns. The routine application rollback therefore retains the
expanded schema. A database downgrade removes the new governance records and
columns and would destroy review/conflict history; it is an emergency destructive
rollback only, never the normal release rollback.

## Security and legal-integrity controls

- Positive verification is capability-gated, optimistic, immutable, and
  independently reviewed.
- Unverified or unhealthy text fails closed in UI, API, drafting, and strategy.
- Redirects are resolved server-side through opaque target identity and revalidated
  at open time; raw provider credentials are never returned.
- Probes do not follow redirects or convert protected/rate-limited responses into
  authoritative content.
- Seed, enrichment, and AI processes cannot overwrite reviewed legal text.
- Conflict evidence remains auditable and quarantine is immediate.
- No external legal filing, provider purchase, source scraping run, legal approval,
  or autonomous legal act was performed or claimed.
