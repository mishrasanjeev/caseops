## Delivered boundary

1. Authorized access managers can inspect current restricted/default policy,
   effective membership/team grants and walls, queued delivery count, policy
   version, explicit exclusions, and soft-revoked history.
2. Every change requires a reason and read-only preview. Preview exposes
   visibility gains/losses, affected memberships, linked-Matter mismatches,
   protected-document count, queued deliveries, and the exact policy version.
3. Apply is step-up protected, locks the docket, rejects stale versions or
   mismatched preview tokens, and atomically commits the canonical row,
   policy-version increment, and correlated audit event.
4. Membership and team grants/walls support effective/expiry windows. Revoke
   records actor/time and increments the row version rather than deleting
   history.
5. Cross-company or inactive subjects, duplicate active rows, and actor
   self-lockout fail closed. Linked Matter permissions are never copied.
6. Queued internal delivery reauthorizes against current IP access immediately
   before dispatch and makes zero external calls after revocation.
7. The IP page exposes the complete internal workflow at 360 px with
   subject-specific revoke names and full-width wrapping controls.

There is no schema change. The workflow uses the target-aware canonical owners
and constraints already migrated by IPLF-026A.
