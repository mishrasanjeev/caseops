## Scope delivered

`GET /api/ip/portfolio/families?grouping=mark|client`

Groups related applications along two axes while proving the members stay
independent, which is what `IP-PROS-11` actually requires:

> Related applications may be grouped by mark/family/client, but each
> jurisdiction/application keeps independent identifiers, events, rules, and
> lifecycle.

- Grouping is **presentational only**. A family carries `member_count`,
  `distinct_jurisdictions` and `distinct_filing_phases` — it deliberately
  exposes **no shared filing phase, deadline or identifier of its own**, and the
  test asserts those keys are absent from the family object.
- Each member keeps its own `application_id`, `docket_id`, `office`,
  `jurisdiction`, `filing_phase`, `lifecycle_version` and `primary_identifier`.
- A client family is labelled from the **Matter**, not from whichever member
  happens to sort first — a docket title names one filing, not a client.
- Records with no mark or no client are counted in `ungrouped_member_count`
  rather than forced into a synthetic family.

## Ownership and duplicate-work analysis

Classification: **EXTEND**. No new table, migration, or writer.

- Reuses the `IPLF-030A` `_scoped_query` and `_deadline_counts` helpers, so
  grouping inherits the same canonical `visible_ip_dockets_filter` access policy
  and the same non-operational-Matter rule. No second access path was written.
- No second prosecution owner: the timeline test exercises the existing
  `IPLF-024` event and workspace routes rather than adding a parallel surface.
- No family table. Grouping is derived at read time from `asset_id` /
  `matter_id`, so there is no new state to keep consistent.

## Explicit caveats

- **Only `IP-PROS-11` is claimed.** `IP-PROS-01..10` and `IP-PROS-12` remain
  co-owned and open on `IPLF-033B`; this slice does not close them even though
  much of their behaviour already exists, because their release evidence and
  remaining facets belong to that slice.
- **No explicit family entity.** Grouping is derived from `asset_id` /
  `matter_id`. The PRD's "family" concept may later need a first-class record
  (priority claims, Madrid basic marks); this slice does not create one and does
  not model priority relationships between members.
- **Local SQLite only.** No real-PostgreSQL CI, no query-plan evidence at
  representative volume, no deployment, no production verification.
- **No frontend, no E2E.** Generated client types exist but nothing consumes them.
- **No pagination on families.** The grouped view materialises all matching
  rows, so a very large portfolio would be unbounded; the flat portfolio route
  is the paginated one.
- Fixtures are synthetic; no real client, mark, proprietor, or registry data.
