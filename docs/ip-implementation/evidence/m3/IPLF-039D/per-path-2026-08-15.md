# IPLF-039D — per-path evidence for UJ-58, and four unimplemented paths

**Slice:** `IPLF-039D`
**Date:** 2026-08-15

## Headline

Writing the per-path tests established that **only two of this slice's six
journey paths are implemented at all**. The slice was recorded
`implemented / passed / deployment_verified` while four of its paths describe
behaviour that does not exist in the codebase.

This is a stronger finding than the 2026-08-14 evidence defect. That audit found
claims resting on unwritten tests. This finds claims resting on **unwritten
features**.

## Proven (2 of 6)

| Path | Proven behaviour |
|---|---|
| `UJ-58-NORMAL` | An incident records severity, summary and structured impact and opens as `open`; verification is refused 409 until containment is recorded; verifying sets `verified`, stores the human-supplied corrective action and a timestamp; closure is per incident and leaves other open incidents untouched. |
| `UJ-58-EXC-02` | Opening or verifying an incident produces **no** notification intent and **no** operational deadline. CaseOps records `remedy_available: uncertain` rather than resolving it, and never supplies a corrective action on the user's behalf. This pins IP-INC-05's no-automated-communication rule so a future regression fails. |

## Not implemented (4 of 6)

Verified by inspection of the code, not inferred from the absence of tests:

| Path | Finding |
|---|---|
| `UJ-58-EXC-01` suspicion disproved but evidence remains | `IpDeadlineIncident.status` is an unconstrained `String` defaulting to `open`, and the only other value the code ever writes is `verified`. There is no disproved, dismissed or closed-without-fault state, so a disproved suspicion cannot be recorded as such. |
| `UJ-58-EXC-03` legal hold blocks deletion | No legal-hold check exists anywhere in the IP incident path. Legal holds belong to `IPLF-028`, which is dry-run only and blocked on approved policy. |
| `UJ-58-EXC-04` different notification decisions per client/insurer | There is no per-recipient decision record. `impact_json` is unstructured free-form, so distinct client, insurer, regulator and court decisions cannot be represented, let alone differ. |
| `UJ-58-EXC-05` platform-wide defect triggers kill switch | No kill switch is reachable from the incident path. |

The incident surface is exactly two routes: create and verify.

## Status effect

- `implementation_status` **downgraded to `in_progress`**. A slice cannot be
  implemented while four of the six paths it owns are unbuilt.
- `verification_status` stays `not_run` and `release_status` stays `blocked`.
- The two proven paths keep their real test references; the four unimplemented
  paths keep `planned:` references, which is now accurate — the test is indeed
  only planned, because the feature is.
- `IP-INC-01` through `IP-INC-08` are **not** claimed. `IP-INC-05` is partially
  evidenced by the abstention test; the rest are open.
