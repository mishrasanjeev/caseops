# IPLF-039E — per-path evidence for UJ-61 chain of title

**Slice:** `IPLF-039E`
**Date:** 2026-08-15

All three journey paths are implemented and are now proven.

| Path | Proven behaviour |
|---|---|
| `UJ-61-NORMAL` | Effective-dated interests form an ordered chain: a closed prior ownership and a later assignment coexist, the successor carries its own effective window, evidence reference and pending recordal, and clean non-overlapping succession produces no conflict flag. A family link is stored on the interest and a docket cannot be its own relation (422). |
| `UJ-61-EXC-01` | A second ownership over an overlapping period is flagged `competing_title`; a licence granted over disputed ownership is flagged and keeps a `pending` recordal rather than being treated as the registered position; a partial-period assignment keeps its own `effective_until` without rewriting the prior interest's dates. |
| `UJ-61-EXC-02` | Recording an assignment on one docket that names another as family leaves the related docket's interests **byte-identical**, including its `recordal_status`. Association is one-directional evidence, never a shared record. |

`IP-POST-02` and `IP-POST-03` are the requirements this slice owns. Neither is
claimed closed: recordal legal basis, form and affected registrations/classes
are not modelled as separate fields, and the interests carry no registry
projection.
