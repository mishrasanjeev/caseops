## Implemented behavior

- Adds tenant-scoped, append-only filing transactions for submission, fee payment, acknowledgement, defect, rejection, corrective resubmission, and acceptance.
- Locks the parent docket/application and requires optimistic lifecycle/application versions.
- Treats the attempt key as the filing-package identity: a second submission for an existing attempt fails, an exact idempotent retry replays, and conflicting reuse fails.
- Leaves submission, fee payment, and acknowledgement in `pre_filing`.
- Makes a defect/rejection immutable and requires a new attempt plus a fresh acknowledgement before acceptance.
- Prevents generic filing-phase PATCHes and direct filing docket events from advancing an operational record.
- Creates the canonical filing event and `filed` phase atomically only from a fully evidenced accepted transaction.
- Enforces capability, entitlement, rollout, tenant, active-lifecycle, and append-only database boundaries.
