`implemented / passed / deployment_verified` while most of the journey paths it

## Proven (3 of 7)

| Path | Proven behaviour |
|---|---|
| `UJ-52-NORMAL` | A legal cost records category, minor-unit amount, currency and evidence reference; with no billing link it is explicitly `unlinked` with null canonical amount and null difference, never assumed matched. The reconciliation report names `matter_billing` as the single accounting owner and carries a 64-character checksum. |
| `UJ-52-EXC-03` | Recording a registry fee paid on the client's behalf raises **no** client invoice — the invoice count is unchanged — creates no billing link, and is reported as awaiting a billing decision rather than as billed. A filing payment is therefore never a client payment. |
| `UJ-52-EXC-06` | A cost pointing at an invoice that does not exist for the Matter reconciles as `missing`, not matched: the link is retained as evidence, the canonical amount and difference stay null, and repeated reconciliation is stable and produces an identical checksum. A voided or absent invoice cannot silently reconcile. |

## Not implemented (4 of 7)

Verified by reading the schema and service, not inferred from missing tests.

### `UJ-52-EXC-01` — the implementation contradicts the requirement

> *No billing Matter blocks billable time/invoicing **but not** nonbillable
> legal-cost capture.*

`add_ip_cost_item` opens with:

```python
if not docket.matter_id:
    raise HTTPException(status_code=409, detail="IP costs require a Matter billing owner.")
```

Every cost is refused when the docket has no Matter — including the nonbillable
legal-cost capture the requirement explicitly protects. This is a **behavioural
defect**, not merely a missing feature: a docket that has not yet been attached
to a billing Matter cannot record an official fee it has genuinely incurred, so
the disbursement fact is lost rather than deferred.

I did not write a test asserting the current 409, because that would entrench
the wrong behaviour.

### `UJ-52-EXC-02` — no exchange conversion exists

`IpCostItem` stores `currency` only. There is no exchange rate, rate source, or
conversion timestamp field anywhere in the schema or service, so "conversion
preserves original amount/rate/source/time" cannot hold. A foreign-currency
associate fee is stored as a bare amount plus a currency code.

### `UJ-52-EXC-04` — no estimate concept exists

The category enum is `official_fee`, `professional_fee`, `associate_fee`,
`disbursement`, `other`. Nothing distinguishes a provider **estimate** from an
actual expense, so an estimated cost and an incurred cost are indistinguishable
in reporting and reconciliation.

### `UJ-52-EXC-05` — no rate confidentiality exists

There is no rate-confidentiality flag, no permissioned rate field, and no
capability check on cost amounts. Confidential rates cannot be restricted.
