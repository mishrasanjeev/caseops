## Duplicate-work reconciliation

The slice adds one `ip_foreign_associate_instructions` coordination aggregate.
It links the existing `IpClientInstruction`, `OutsideCounsel`, approved Matter
assignment, `Communication`, `IpDocument`, `IpCostItem`,
`OutsideCounselSpendRecord`, `IpDeadline`, `IpDocketEvent`, access and audit
owners. It does not add another associate directory, mailer, document store,
invoice/spend/cost ledger, deadline or notification engine, access system or
legal event log.

IPLF-059B retains the complete UJ-37 operator page, reminders, notifications,
guide, landing-page claims and browser acceptance. No journey path is claimed
complete by this administrative foundation slice.

## Implemented contract

- Tenant/access-filtered list, create, detail, workspace and transaction APIs.
- Versioned instruction threads with optimistic aggregate and docket lifecycle
  concurrency, append-only events and audit lineage.
- Canonical client authority or explicit external authority evidence, active
  panel/jurisdiction and approved Matter-assignment checks.
- Docket-linked document selection with explicit privileged-document approval.
- Estimate, currency, tax, assumptions and assignment-budget checks; approver
  evidence for later fee/FX/tax changes.
- Canonical Communication dispatch with exact associate-recipient validation,
  separate delivery and acknowledgement facts, and external-dispatch fallback.
- Query, lawyer-approved substantive response, refusal and successor
  reassignment transactions that preserve correspondence and version history.
- Foreign filing report plus independent verification evidence and filters for
  outstanding acknowledgements and filings without independent proof.
- Actual-cost, associate-invoice/spend and client-billing links with amount,
  currency, paid-state and matched-reconciliation completion gates.
- Additive migration with PostgreSQL lock timeout, empty rollback and
  restore-forward refusal after legal data exists.
