## Ownership and duplicate-work review

IPLF-059B extends the IPLF-059A instruction service. It does not add another
associate directory, assignment system, mailer, document store, financial
ledger, deadline engine, notification scheduler, permission model or event log.
It links `OutsideCounsel`, Matter assignments and spend, `Communication`,
`IpDocument`, `IpCostItem`, Matter invoices, `IpDeadline`, docket events, access
and audit through their existing public APIs and service owners.

Reminders use `NotificationDeliveryIntent` and its established channel,
idempotency, delivery and cancellation semantics. The foreign-associate service
owns only the instruction policy and source linkage.

## Implemented workflow

- Added `/app/ip/foreign-associates` with all, outstanding-acknowledgement and
  missing-independent-evidence queues.
- Added instruction intake from an application/search reference, client
  authority, target jurisdiction, active/preferred associate, approved Matter
  assignment and budget, estimate/currency/tax terms, scoped fields and
  same-docket document selection.
- Privileged/internal documents remain excluded unless explicitly selected by
  an `ip:approve` user.
- Added approve, dispatch, acknowledge, query, lawyer-approved response, fee
  change, filing report, independent evidence verification, invoice linkage,
  completion, refusal, cancellation and reassignment controls.
- Delivery and acknowledgement are rendered separately. Foreign filing ID,
  evidence status, paid invoice state, source links, documents and immutable
  transaction history remain visible.
- Added idempotent acknowledgement reminders at bounded offsets and a critical
  overdue escalation through the shared notification owner. Acknowledgement,
  refusal, cancellation and reassignment cancel actionable reminders without
  deleting provider-disabled or delivered audit history.
- Added generated OpenAPI types, navigation, user guide, truthful law-firm
  landing copy and exact-release production acceptance wiring.
