## Delivered contract

- Tenant-owned links match one trademark application or IP proceeding to an
  office identity and require an explicit confirm, mismatch, or retire decision.
- Durable, payload-bound idempotent attempts record success, no-change,
  redacted failure, blocked status, attribution, correlation, and whether an
  external call occurred.
- Raw and normalized snapshots are hashed, source-attributed, size-bounded,
  append-only in SQLite and PostgreSQL, and corrected through a single
  immutable successor rather than overwrite or deletion.
- Field diffs distinguish `added`, `changed`, and `removed`; high-risk status,
  proprietor, opposition, refusal, cancellation, and deadline facts require IP
  approval before acceptance.
- Accepted changes emit candidate and reconciled IP docket events. Deadline
  fields carry an explicit recalculation marker. Reject, map, and defer remain
  reviewable decisions with actor, reason, time, and optimistic versioning.
- No-change snapshots advance freshness and history. Failures retain the last
  good snapshot and accepted legal state.
- Court/CNR references reuse existing Matter bookmarks and `TrackedCase`
  records. Court updates and provider snapshots are never copied into registry
  persistence.

## Data and migration proof

Migration `20260824_0002` is the sole Alembic head and adds five governed
tables: `ip_registry_links`, `ip_registry_sync_attempts`,
`ip_registry_snapshots`, `ip_registry_diffs`, and `ip_tracked_case_links`.
Composite foreign keys enforce tenant identity for legal targets, actors,
attempt replay, correction lineage, emitted events, and tracked cases. Empty
round trip, destructive-downgrade refusal, append-only triggers, and populated
snapshot retention are covered by migration tests. A PostgreSQL test asserts
the same foreign keys and immutable trigger behavior in CI.
