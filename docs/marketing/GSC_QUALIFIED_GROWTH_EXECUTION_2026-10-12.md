## Three Practical Demo Scorecards

Use a local synthetic workspace for recordings. A real buyer conversation
requires consent and excludes confidential case information from public assets.

**Law-firm walkthrough:** identify one matter and owner; inspect dated tasks,
source documents and hearing information; demonstrate a handoff and permission
boundary; show the supported billing workflow and current limitations; confirm
provider funding and court coverage before promising unattended refresh.
Ask which handoff or follow-up becomes difficult today and what the buyer
would need to see to accept a scoped pilot.

**General-counsel walkthrough:** identify supported matter types; inspect an
authorized portfolio and accountability; show source/history access boundaries;
check available outside-counsel coordination rather than imply an unverified
integration; discuss deployment and security evidence separately from marketing.
Ask which management decision the team cannot currently answer reliably and
what reporting/access proof is needed for evaluation.

**Solo-lawyer walkthrough:** organize a small synthetic matter with its next
action, documents and dates; inspect mobile controls; distinguish manually
recorded hearings from licensed provider updates; inspect a review-first draft
only under eligible source/budget policy; verify current plan terms. Ask which
daily follow-up is hardest to keep consistent. Do not promise minutes saved,
winning cases or universally correct AI text.
