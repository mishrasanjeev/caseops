## Required Reconciliation

The annual memorandum distinguishes closed office holidays from personal
restricted leave. It also allows specified later date changes and different
outside-Delhi selections. Therefore it cannot be copied unchanged into a
single nationwide IP calendar. The April and September additions already show
why an annual PDF alone is insufficient. Its publication date is not the
retrieval date or proof that no later amendment exists.

Before activating a calendar, reconcile its exact registry office and year,
applicable annual annexure, later closure/date-change notices, weekends,
exceptional working days and effective bounds. Retain each source edition and
hash with the candidate. An authorized proposer and a different real authorized
approver must perform the existing calendar workflow. Synthetic test identities
are never independent production reviewers.

A deadline rule separately requires the actual operative provision and trigger,
jurisdiction/office/effective bounds, reviewed deterministic fixtures,
independent fixture review, legal activation and current company-policy
selection. A renewal chapter is not evidence for an unrelated default
examination-response rule. General statutory renewal periods do not establish
one registration's current renewal date without its current registration and
subsequent source history.

## Portfolio Date Verification Gap

The current portfolio date reader/writer supports missing, manual-unverified and
imported-unverified facts. It has no source-specific verification command.
The reserved response enum value `verified` does not supply that authority.
An accepted manually supplied registry snapshot, a linked document or a
confirmed but separate calculated deadline must not silently promote the date.

A future positive verification must identify the actual registration, exact
source version/hash, date field and value, current access/lifecycle and the real
authorized review, with stale/mismatched/revoked evidence rejected. No genuine
registration-specific source was established by these general publications.
IP15-009 therefore remains an explicit capability/evidence gap, not a fake
positive fixture or an asserted fixed production defect.
